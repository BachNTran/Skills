# sensing · requirements

- **REQ-SENS-001** (parents: REQ-FW-001) — Provide a humidity reading every 60 s, marked invalid on a bus or CRC error.
- **REQ-SENS-002** (parents: REQ-FW-002) — Raise LOW_BATTERY when the battery stays below 3300 mV for 1 s.
